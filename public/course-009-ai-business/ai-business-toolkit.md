# AI Business Toolkit

## Opportunity Canvas
Customer segment / painful repeated task / current workaround / measurable outcome / why AI helps / what stays human / evidence / smallest test / maximum test budget / go-revise-stop decision.

## Prompt Framework
Objective → Context → Source material → Constraints → Output format → Human verification.

## Client Brief
Business / audience / offer / current process / pain / examples / prohibited claims / deadline / approver / success metric.

## AI QA Checklist
- No invented facts or citations.
- Confidential data handled appropriately.
- Numbers recalculated independently.
- Brand voice checked.
- Output human-edited.
- Deliverable tested in the real workflow.

## Outreach Framework
Specific observation → useful idea → relevant proof/sample → small next step. Keep it personal; do not mass-spam.

## Monthly Review
Leads, replies, calls, won jobs, average price, delivery hours, variable costs, refunds, contribution profit, repeated customer requests, next experiment.
