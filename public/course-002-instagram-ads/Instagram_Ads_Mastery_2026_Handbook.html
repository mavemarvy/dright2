Operating principles

  • Start from the business outcome, not the Boost button.
  • Build creative for the placement and mobile screen.
  • Predefine target CPA, test budget and decision rules.
  • Keep ad promise and landing/message destination consistent.
  • Use business records to validate attributed revenue.

Capstone

Build a complete campaign plan containing customer research, offer, objective, audience, Feed/Stories/Reels creative, tracking, budget, KPI targets, stop rules and scale rules.
