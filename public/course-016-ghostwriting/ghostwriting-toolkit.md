# Ghostwriting Client Kit

## Client Brief
Format / target reader / purpose / client voice / source material / interviews / length range / deadline / approval chain / prohibited claims / confidentiality.

## Interview Guide
Background, turning points, examples, failures, lessons, opinions, evidence, reader question, desired action and details to verify.

## Voice Guide
Sentence length, vocabulary, tone, humour, formality, phrases to use or avoid, point of view and sample paragraph.

## Project Scope
Research hours, interviews, word count, milestones, revision rounds, rights or credit, confidentiality, payment schedule and exclusions.

## Revision Log
Request / correction or new scope / decision / owner / due date / completed version.

## Handoff
Files, final word count, open fact notes, rights or attribution notes, source files included and next step.
