# China Importation Toolkit

## Product Opportunity Scorecard
Score 1–5: demand evidence, target-price fit, margin potential, size/weight, fragility, regulatory complexity, defect risk, repeat purchase, competition and after-sales burden.

## Supplier RFQ
Specification / quantity / MOQ / sample / unit price / packaging / customization / lead time / quotation basis / payment terms / evidence requested / destination warehouse.

## Supplier Verification
Identity, product specialization, platform history, documents, current product video, sample result, payment consistency, communication quality and red flags.

## Quality Checklist
Critical, major and minor defects; dimensions; materials; colour; packaging; quantity; labels; photos/video required.

## Freight Comparison
Forwarder / route / air-sea / charging basis / origin cost / freight / clearing scope / destination / prohibited items / transit / insurance / total quoted cost.

## Import Decision Gate
Do not pay until demand, supplier evidence, sample/QC, current regulatory status, landed cost, cash buffer and resale plan are acceptable.
