WhatsApp Marketing & Sales Mastery 2026

DRIGHT Course 003 offline handbook.

Purpose: Build a structured WhatsApp Business sales system without relying on spam or unsupported income promises.

15-module roadmap

  1. Foundations
  2. Professional profile setup
  3. Catalogs
  4. Lead capture
  5. Labels and CRM
  6. Quick replies
  7. Broadcasts and consent
  8. Status marketing
  9. Sales scripts
  10. Objection handling
  11. Follow-up
  12. Retention and referrals
  13. Click-to-WhatsApp ads
  14. Team workflows and automation
  15. Policy, capstone and 90-day plan

Operating principles

  • Use WhatsApp Business with a clear customer journey.
  • Collect permission and keep broadcasts relevant.
  • Organize leads with labels and daily follow-up routines.
  • Use automation for repetitive information, not empathy.
  • Track response time, qualified conversations, close rate and repeat sales.
