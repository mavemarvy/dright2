# Affiliate Marketing Kit — WhatsApp Marketing & Sales Mastery 2026

## Core angle
Turn WhatsApp Business into an organized sales channel instead of a crowded inbox.

## Hooks
- Lots of WhatsApp chats but very few sales?
- Learn catalogs, labels, Status, broadcasts, follow-ups and closing.
- Build a WhatsApp sales system that works from your phone.

## Audience
Small business owners, online vendors, freelancers, service providers, creators and sales teams.

## Compliance
Do not promise guaranteed income. Do not encourage purchased contact lists, scraped numbers or spam. Respect WhatsApp Business policy and customer opt-outs.
